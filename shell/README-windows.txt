FrogComposband with the new interface
=====================================

To play: unzip this folder anywhere (for example D:\Games\FrogComposband) and
double-click FrogComposband.exe. Windows may ask once whether to run it.

Keys: every key of the original game works as before. F11 (or Alt+Enter)
switches full screen. Closing the window saves the game first.

Mouse: click a square to walk there, click next to you to step or attack,
right-click a creature or item for its details, roll the wheel over the map
to zoom. Click any lettered line in a menu to choose it.

Your characters are saved in game\lib\save. To move to a newer build, copy
that folder across.
